Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a vehicle?

Simply search for a vehicle, select your dates, and proceed to booking. Choose self-drive or hire a driver.

Is security deposit refundable?

Yes, security deposit is fully refundable after vehicle inspection, subject to no damage claims.

What is your damage policy?

All damage is evidence-based with photos. Customers can dispute claims through our transparent process.

How do I list my vehicle?

Complete our verification process, upload documents and photos, set pricing, and publish your listing.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, Debit/Credit Cards, and Net Banking. UPI is our primary payment method.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes, cancellations are allowed with our cancellation policy. Check terms before booking.
